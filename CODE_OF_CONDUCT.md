# Code of Conduct

RepoReady contributors must keep project spaces useful and safe.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal traits.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer asks.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. Report conduct problems privately through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed without public disclosure
unless the reporter agrees.
